Week 13-1 · Task 4

Course feedback form

Task: Video lecture practice: a feedback form with Student name (required), Student ID (8 digits, required), a star rating (radio buttons, required), topics covered well (checkboxes, optional) and comments (max 300 characters, live count). Validate on submit and thank the student by name. Bonus: disable Submit while a required field is empty.

How it works

  • Text inputs, the textarea and the radio buttons share one handleChange; radios in a group share the same name.
  • The topics are an array inside the form object, updated with the spread and .filter().
  • The ID field only keeps digits (replace(/D/g, "")) and is checked with /^d{8}$/.
  • The comment counter comes from form.comments.length, and maxLength stops it at 300.
  • Bonus: disabled={missingRequired} keeps Submit greyed out until name, ID and rating are filled in.
