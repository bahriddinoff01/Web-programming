Live character counter

Task: Study guide, exercise 1: a controlled textarea with a maximum of 200 characters. Show a live count (X / 200) below it, and disable the textarea when the limit is reached.

Live result

Loading…

How it works

  • The textarea is controlled: value={bio} and onChange saves every change in state.
  • e.target.value.slice(0, MAX) cuts anything past 200 characters, even when a long text is pasted.
  • The count turns red when fewer than 20 characters are left, and the textarea is disabled at the limit.
  • A disabled textarea can't be edited, so a button removes the last 20 characters to let the user continue.
