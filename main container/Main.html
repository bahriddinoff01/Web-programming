About me

I'm a Computer Engineering student at Gachon University. I got into web development because I wanted to understand how the sites and apps I use every day actually work, and now I build them myself.

This semester I went from my first HTML page to apps that load live data from APIs, forms with full validation, accessibility audits and React components. I also worked in a team on TravelAI, an AI travel planner.

I care about clean code and about sites that work for everyone: on phones, with a keyboard, and with a screen reader.
