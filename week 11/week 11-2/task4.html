Interactive shopping cart

Task: Homework: a product list of at least 5 items (name, price, image URL), Add / Remove buttons, a cart sidebar with the total computed from state, + and − quantity controls per item, all inside a ShoppingCart component.

How it works

  • ShoppingCart is the parent: it owns the cart state (an array of items) and passes data and functions down to ProductList and CartPanel.
  • Adding a product that is already in the cart raises its quantity with .map() and the spread; a new product is added with [...prev, …].
  • Remove uses .filter(); − lowers the quantity and an item that reaches 0 leaves the cart.
  • The total and the item count are computed from state with .reduce() every render, never stored separately.
