Shopping counter

Task: Video lecture challenge: three items, each with − and + buttons for its quantity. A quantity can't go below 0, and the total is quantity × price.

How it works

  • All three quantities live in one array in state, [0, 0, 0], in the same order as ITEMS.
  • changeQuantity(index, step) uses .map() to change only that item; Math.max(0, qty + step) stops it going below 0.
  • The − button is also disabled at 0, so it is clear nothing more can be removed.
  • The total is calculated with .reduce() on every render, so it can never get out of sync with the quantities.
